# Time Off Policy

This is a fictional, synthetic time-off policy for the Northlight
Robotics RAG demo corpus.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Northlight uses an unlimited PTO framework: there is no fixed accrual
balance, but every employee is expected to take at least 15 days off per
calendar year, tracked by your manager during 1:1s. PTO requests go
through the People Ops portal and need manager approval for anything
longer than five consecutive days.

## Public holidays

Employees in Portugal observe 13 public holidays per year; employees in
Kenya observe the local public holiday calendar for Nairobi. The full
list is published each January in the People Ops portal.

## Parental leave

Parental leave (16 weeks for the primary caregiver, 8 weeks for the
secondary caregiver) is paid out separately from PTO and does not draw
down your PTO balance. Employees can also use ordinary PTO before or
after parental leave to extend time away from work; see the Benefits
document for full parental leave eligibility rules.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is unlimited and does not require manager approval for the
first three consecutive days. Absences longer than three days require a
note from People Ops confirming the leave.
