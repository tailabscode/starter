# Mitigation Strategies

## Diversifying Suppliers

The most direct mitigation is qualifying more than one supplier per mineral and per region, so a
single mine, refinery, or gigafactory outage does not stop production. This is slower and more
expensive than single-sourcing but is now standard practice among large automakers.

## Strategic Stockpiles

Some automakers and battery makers hold several months of buffer inventory of refined battery
material, similar to strategic petroleum reserves, specifically to absorb a short supply
disruption without stopping vehicle production.

## Vertical Integration

A few automakers have taken direct equity stakes in mining or refining companies, or built their
own cell manufacturing, to gain more control over supply and pricing rather than relying entirely
on external suppliers under long-term contracts.
