# Ingest Pipeline Notes

This is a fictional, synthetic document written for a multimodal RAG
demo corpus.

## What the schematic shows

`schematic_pipeline.png` is a minimal schematic of three grey boxes
connected left to right by short black lines, representing how a scan
moves through the system: the drone's camera, an edge-compute box that
runs the first-pass shelf-detection model, and cloud storage where the
final inventory count lands.

## Why it matters

The schematic has no chart bars and almost no color at all -- it is
mostly a white background with grey boxes and black connector lines,
which makes it easy to distinguish from the three revenue charts by
color alone, even without reading any labels.
