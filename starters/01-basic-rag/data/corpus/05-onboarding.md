# New Hire Onboarding

This is a fictional, synthetic onboarding guide for the Northlight
Robotics RAG demo corpus.

## Before day one

IT ships a laptop and provisions VPN credentials per the Remote Work
Policy document's security requirements. People Ops sends a benefits
enrollment link so you can review the health insurance and retirement
plan options described in the Benefits document before you start.

## First week checklist

In your first week: enroll in health insurance through the People Ops
portal, set up the company VPN and password manager, meet your assigned
onboarding buddy, and submit your home office equipment stipend claim if
you are a remote employee (see the Remote Work Policy and Expense Policy
documents for how that claim works).

## 30-60-90 day plan

By day 30, new engineers ship a small fix to the fleet-management
backend. By day 60, new hires in any department have completed at least
one full project cycle with their team. By day 90, your manager reviews
your onboarding plan with you and confirms you're ready for standard
performance review cycles.

## Buddy system

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from their department
for the first 90 days, distinct from their manager, as an informal point
of contact for day-to-day questions.
