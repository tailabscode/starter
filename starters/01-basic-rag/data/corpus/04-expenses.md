# Expense and Travel Policy

This is a fictional, synthetic expense policy for the Northlight Robotics
RAG demo corpus.

## What counts as a reimbursable expense

Reasonable costs for customer travel, conference attendance, client
meals, and approved software subscriptions are reimbursable. Personal
meals on a normal working day are not, except during approved travel.

## Travel booking

Flights and hotels for customer site visits are booked through the
company travel portal. Economy class is standard for flights under six
hours; anything longer may be booked in premium economy with manager
approval.

## Submitting expenses

Expenses are submitted through the internal expense tool with a receipt
attached, within 30 days of the purchase. One-time hardware purchases
under the home office equipment stipend (see the Remote Work Policy
document) are submitted through this same tool, tagged as "equipment
stipend" rather than "travel."

## Approval thresholds

Expenses under $200 are auto-approved. Expenses between $200 and $1,000
require manager approval. Anything over $1,000 requires both manager and
People Ops approval.
