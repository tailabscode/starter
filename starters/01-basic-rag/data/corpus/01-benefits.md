# Benefits

This is a fictional, synthetic benefits summary for the Northlight
Robotics RAG demo corpus.

## Health insurance

Northlight covers 100% of the employee premium and 70% of dependent
premiums for the company's PPO health plan, effective on day one of
employment. Enrollment happens during onboarding; see the Onboarding
document for the exact steps and deadline.

## Retirement plan

Northlight offers a defined-contribution retirement plan with a 4%
company match, vested immediately. Enrollment is optional and can be
changed at any time through the People Ops portal.

## Wellness stipend

Every employee receives a $50 per month wellness stipend, reimbursable
for gym memberships, therapy co-pays, or fitness equipment. This is
separate from the one-time home office equipment stipend described in
the Remote Work Policy document -- the wellness stipend is a recurring
monthly benefit, not a hardware purchase.

## Parental leave

Northlight provides 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary
caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver, available to any
employee who has completed 90 days of employment. Parental leave is
administered by People Ops and is paid out separately from your PTO
balance -- see the Time Off Policy document for how parental leave
interacts with PTO accrual and public holidays.
