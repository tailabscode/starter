# Remote Work Policy

This is a fictional, synthetic remote-work policy for the Northlight
Robotics RAG demo corpus.

## Who can work remotely

Engineering, Customer Success, and People Ops roles are remote-eligible
by default. Field Operations roles require on-site presence at customer
warehouses and are not remote-eligible, though the associated People Ops
and dispatch coordination work is.

## Home office equipment stipend

Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office equipment stipend to
cover a desk, chair, monitor, or other hardware, claimed through the
expense system within the first 60 days of starting. This is a one-time
hardware stipend and is separate from the recurring $50 per month
wellness stipend described in the Benefits document.

## Security requirements

Remote employees must enable full-disk encryption, connect to internal
systems only through the company VPN, and use a company-managed password
manager. IT provisions VPN credentials before day one; see the Onboarding
document for the pre-day-one checklist.

## Core collaboration hours

All remote employees, regardless of timezone, keep 10:00-13:00 UTC free
for synchronous meetings. Outside that window, work hours are flexible.
