# Northlight Robotics Employee Handbook

This handbook is a fictional, synthetic document written for a RAG demo.
Northlight Robotics is not a real company. Any resemblance to real
policies is coincidental.

## About Northlight Robotics

Northlight Robotics was founded in 2019 and builds autonomous inspection
drones used by warehouse operators to scan inventory shelves overnight.
The company is headquartered in Porto, Portugal, with a smaller
engineering hub in Nairobi, Kenya. Northlight has roughly 140 employees
across four departments: Engineering, Field Operations, Customer Success,
and People Ops.

## Mission and values

Our mission is to make warehouse inventory counts boring: accurate,
automatic, and something nobody has to think about. We hold three values
above the rest:

- **Ship something real.** Prototypes are worthless until a customer's
  drone fleet has flown them.
- **Write it down.** If a decision matters, it belongs in a document, not
  only in someone's memory.
- **Default to trust.** We hire adults and treat them like adults,
  including with unlimited PTO and flexible remote work.

## Org structure

Engineering owns the drone firmware, the fleet-management backend, and the
computer-vision models used for shelf scanning. Field Operations installs
and maintains customer drone docks. Customer Success handles onboarding
and support tickets. People Ops runs hiring, benefits, and this handbook.

New hires should read the Benefits, Time Off, Remote Work, and Onboarding
sections of this handbook in their first week; see the Onboarding
document for the full first-week checklist.
