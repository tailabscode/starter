# Geopolitical Risk in the Battery Supply Chain

## Export Controls

Several governments have introduced export controls or licensing requirements on battery-grade
minerals and processing technology. These controls can be tightened with little notice, which is
different from a normal commodity price shock because it is a policy decision, not a market one.

## Refining Concentration

Mining is more geographically spread out than refining. A large share of global lithium and
cobalt refining capacity, where raw ore is turned into battery-grade material, is concentrated
in a small number of countries, which creates a bottleneck even when the raw ore itself comes
from a more diverse set of mines.

## Trade Agreements

Some automakers now qualify for tax incentives only if a minimum share of battery minerals were
extracted or processed in countries with a free trade agreement, which has started to reshape
sourcing decisions independent of raw cost.
