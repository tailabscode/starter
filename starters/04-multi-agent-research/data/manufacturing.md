# Battery Manufacturing Risk

## Gigafactory Concentration

Battery cell manufacturing is dominated by a small number of very large "gigafactories," most
of them in China. A single facility can supply battery cells for hundreds of thousands of
vehicles a year, which means a fire, labor dispute, or export restriction at one plant can
create a shortage felt across multiple automakers at once.

## Quality and Safety

Manufacturing defects in battery cells are a leading cause of vehicle recalls related to
electric vehicles. Automakers increasingly require in-line defect detection and cell-level
traceability from suppliers, not just a certificate of quality at the end of the production run.

## Regional Diversification

Several governments now offer subsidies for battery manufacturing built domestically, which is
starting to shift new factory investment toward North America and Europe, though the majority
of existing capacity remains concentrated in East Asia.
