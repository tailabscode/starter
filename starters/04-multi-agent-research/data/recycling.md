# Battery Recycling and Second Life

## Why Recycling Matters for Risk

Recycled battery material does not depend on new mining, so a mature recycling industry reduces
exposure to the mining and geopolitical risks that raw material sourcing carries. Recovered
lithium, cobalt, and nickel can be reused in new cells at purity levels comparable to freshly
mined material.

## Current Recycling Rates

Recycling rates for electric vehicle batteries remain low relative to the volume that will need
processing once today's vehicles reach end of life, mostly because large volumes of batteries
have not yet aged out of service. Recycling capacity is being built well ahead of that expected
wave of retired batteries.

## Second-Life Use

Before recycling, a used vehicle battery often still has enough capacity for less demanding
applications such as stationary grid storage, which delays the need for recycling and spreads
the mineral demand of a single battery across a longer effective lifetime.
